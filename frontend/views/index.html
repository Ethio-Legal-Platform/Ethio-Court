Constitutional Architecture

Federal Court Hierarchy & Divisions

Established under the Constitution of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia (FDRE) to uphold the rule of law and deliver impartial justice.

Tier 1 · Apex Court
የፌዴራል ጠቅላይ ፍርድ ቤት
Federal Supreme Court (FSC)

The highest judicial organ led by the Chief Justice and Deputy Chief Justice. Exercises final appellate jurisdiction and original jurisdiction over high-level international cases.

  • Cassation Division (የሰበር ሰሚ ችሎት)
  • Binding Legal Precedent Authority
  • Presidential & Judicial Council Leadership
  • Final Federal Appellate Review
Tier 2 · Appellate & Trial
የፌዴራል ከፍተኛ ፍርድ ቤት
Federal High Court (FHC)

Handles complex civil, commercial, criminal, labor, and administrative disputes. Operates specialized benches across Lideta, Arada, and regional circuit divisions.

  • Lideta Civil & Commercial Benches
  • Arada Criminal & Constitutional Division
  • Labor, Tax & Customs Benches
  • Dire Dawa & Regional Circuit Benches
Tier 3 · Primary Jurisdiction
የፌዴራል የመጀመሪያ ደረጃ ፍርድ ቤት
Federal First Instance Court

The primary trial court for initial civil claims, petty offenses, and family or contract disputes across federal sub-cities and administrative zones.

  • Sub-City & District Trial Benches
  • Citizen Intake & E-Filing Hubs
  • Initial Evidence Indexing
  • Summary Civil & Criminal Proceedings
